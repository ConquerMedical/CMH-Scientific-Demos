NPIS · Nonparametric Information Sufficiency

Determines which distinctions a dataset contains for a declared task, on realized data, before probability or model estimation.

CHARACTERIZED

—
PBR · local branching—0 determined · 1 maximally opposed
PIM · branch separation—>1 = separated opposed futures
FAI · level share—share attributable to level vs motion
RPA · predictive availability—directional information above trivial
TB · temporal balance—level vs slope dominance
NI20—20% branching criterion
Opposed NI—opposite-direction support
Effective transitions—current stress-test fraction
PBRWhether nearby observed states still lead to multiple directional branches.
PIMHow far apart the compatible future branches are.
TB / FAIWhether recent motion adds information beyond accumulated level.
RPAHow much directional information is available in the substrate above a trivial information algebra.
DR / CSOptional law/departure diagnostics where a regime or intervention is present; they are not the same object as sufficiency.
Final reportNPIS returns a profile and information statement, not a single universal “dataset quality” score.
Frozen real-data basis. The public NPIS table reports construct-level PBR, PIM, TB/FAI and RPA on ADNI and MIMIC-IV. The wider longitudinal program also evaluates NI20/opposed-future structure across ADNI, PPMI, PRO-ACT ALS, HRS and Add Health.
