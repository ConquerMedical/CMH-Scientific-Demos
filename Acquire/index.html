Decision boundary

SUPPORTED: choose among tested candidate observations when held-out evidence shows a measurable improvement.

NOT SUPPORTED: claim that any untested sensor, assay or modality will resolve the information deficit.

UNRESOLVED is valid: when no tested option justifies a recommendation, the system should say so.
