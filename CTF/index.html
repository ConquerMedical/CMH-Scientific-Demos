public demonstration logic
CTF · Clinical Trial Falsifier
CMH Scientific public demonstration. The adjustable cohort is illustrative; CLS is a validated metric demonstrated on ALS PRO-ACT. Not a power calculator, trial-registration system, or clinical decision tool.
CTF asks a direct pre-design question: does the apparent responder pattern survive when the same subgroup procedure is run on placebo, or is the pattern reproducible prediction error?

Validation & data provenance

The adjustable cohort on this page is illustrative. The falsification / CLS evidence is grounded in the pooled PRO-ACT ALS placebo-control experiment reported on Research Square.

PRO-ACT ALS clinical-trial validation2,445 patients · 54,007 landmark–horizon pairs
Held-out horizons60, 90, and 180 days; active- and placebo-arm prognostic laws evaluated in held-out patients
Negative-control testThe same apparent responder/harm procedure was applied to placebo patients to measure its false-positive behavior
Boundary: the sliders generate an illustrative response field so a viewer can see the logic move. They are not the validation cohort. The empirical falsification basis is PRO-ACT; no individual treatment effect is inferred from prognostic residuals alone.
