Method and provenance
  • Zoom and cut-away (pinch, the + and − buttons, or the mouse wheel after clicking the view; ◐ slices the tumor open) enlarge the equivalent-volume view to show how its compartments nest. They do not reveal detail finer than the MRI, whose resolution is on the millimetre scale.
  • Data: LUMIERE longitudinal glioblastoma dataset (Suter et al., Scientific Data 9:768, 2022): DeepBraTumIA compartment volumes, expert RANO ratings with written rationale, demographics, MGMT/IDH status and overall survival as provided.
  • Matching: a search over rated scans from patients represented each scan by radiomic features reduced to principal components, and paired scans with similar states whose next expert ratings went opposite ways. "Closer than X% of scan pairs" refers to that representation. It does not establish identical biology.
  • Look inside shows illustrative cells, not imaged cells: the MRI cannot resolve individual cells. The animation rate is driven by each patient's measured compartment-level rate of change (enhancing volume for the rim, necrotic volume for the core boundary, edema/FLAIR volume for the infiltrating edge). It is not a measurement or estimate of cellular division, death or migration rates. Cell appearance and the zone layout follow published glioblastoma histology (pseudopalisading cells around necrosis, microvascular proliferation near the enhancing rim, tumor cells infiltrating the non-enhancing region); cell counts are not calibrated to a density.
  • Between scans the volumes follow shape-preserving interpolation; only the dots are measurements. Shapes are equivalent-volume spheres sized by each compartment, identical in texture for both patients so only size differs.
  • Not in these data: treatment details after surgery. Differences in treatment, timing since surgery, age or earlier history could explain the divergence; this demo shows that the matched snapshot alone did not predict it.
  • Expert ratings can be driven by findings that total volume does not capture, such as T2 progression or new non-target lesions. Rationale text is shown as provided.
  • Overall survival is the dataset's value in weeks; the panel dims when it is reached.
  • Automated segmentation volumes can fluctuate between scans for technical reasons.
  • Patient-071's first scan has no rating in the dataset; it is labelled "before surgery" because it precedes the same-week post-operative scan.
