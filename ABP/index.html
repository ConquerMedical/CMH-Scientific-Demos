Why individual-first?

Tested head-to-head in type 1 diabetes.

We gave a population-based, digital-twin-style model and ABP the same problem: identify an intervention that would move high blood glucose toward a target. One started from the population. ABP started from each person's own biological history.

DIGITAL-TWIN-STYLE POPULATION MODEL
24.5%

of individual-dynamics intervention choices matched

The population-based model reproduced the intervention selected from the patient's own dynamics only 24.5% of the time. In the other 75.5% of tests, the population-based answer differed from the intervention derived from that individual's own biological history.

ABP · INDIVIDUAL-FIRST MODEL
96.0%

of tested high-glucose cases moved toward target

Across 400 high-glucose cases, ABP found a clinically constrained intervention that moved the individual's modeled biological state in the desired direction 96.0% of the time.

A model personalized from the population is not the same thing as a model built from the person. The diabetes experiment showed that the two approaches can look at the same problem and reach very different conclusions about what intervention fits the individual.
Research demonstrator. LUMIERE imaging is de-identified research data. Patient-derived observations and modeled counterfactuals are labeled separately. ABP outputs are retrospective, non-causal experimental hypotheses and are not treatment recommendations.
