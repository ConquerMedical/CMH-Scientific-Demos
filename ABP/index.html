Why radiation can worsen the margin. Sublethal irradiation has been shown repeatedly to increase glioma cell migration and invasiveness, through αvβ3 integrin and MMP-2/9 (Wild-Bode, Cancer Res 2001), p53–MMP-2 (2015), HIF1α/JMY in glioma stem-like cells at doses as low as 0.5 Gy (2020), and CXCL12/KCa3.1 (2019). Irradiated glioma produces more tumour satellites in vivo, and most relapses arise adjacent to the irradiated field. The effect is consistently reported across cell lines, stem-like cells and in vivo models, with at least one co-culture study reporting the opposite.

Literature-grounded ABP demonstrator. The primitive encodes published qualitative relationships among glioblastoma growth, radiation exposure, alkylating therapy, MGMT status, treatment-associated necrosis, pseudoprogression, and infiltrative response. Numerical parameterization is illustrative and is not fitted to an individual patient. The demonstrator illustrates biological-primitive perturbation, target-directed inverse interrogation, intervention design, and response-modifier exploration; it does not provide treatment recommendations. These are not patient images and no restricted data are distributed here.
