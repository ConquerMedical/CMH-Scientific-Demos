Method and provenance
  • Zoom and cut-away (pinch, the + and − buttons, or the mouse wheel after clicking the view; ◐ slices the tumor open) enlarge the equivalent-volume model to show how its compartments nest. They do not reveal detail finer than the MRI, whose resolution is on the millimetre scale.
  • Cells (✺ switches between cells and the original measurement dots) are illustrative renderings in the style of fluorescence microscopy, not imaged cells. Each compartment's cells sit within its measured boundary, so they grow and shrink with the measured volumes, and the number shown in each compartment scales with its share of the tumor. Shapes, motion and colors are illustrative. At normal view the measured compartments and their outlines dominate; zooming in brings the cells forward. The outlines follow each compartment's measured shape and break where tissue is removed.
  • Starting state: measured LUMIERE Patient-067 compartments at week 156 (progressive disease). The week 157 post-operative scan is shown for comparison only; it was not used to fit the model.
  • 3-D body: equivalent-volume geometry with radii proportional to the cube root of each compartment volume; the surface shape is a fixed stylization. The cut shows the chosen approach; volumes shrink by the modeled removal fraction.
  • The ring is a demonstration risk map around the tumor with two high-risk sectors. It stands in for patient-specific eloquent-cortex and tract mapping, which this dataset does not provide. Its values are not clinical risk estimates.
  • Removal fraction = extent × depth. Wider and deeper resections add reactive regrowth near the cavity and raise the corridor score; the score sets clear (<0.22), borderline (<0.36), or high-risk.
  • The search tries all extents, depths and directions in 5° steps and returns the largest removal that stays clear.
