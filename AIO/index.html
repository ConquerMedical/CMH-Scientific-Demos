Give AIO a scientific literature. It discovers the structure.

AIO is a general platform for converting a field's claims into auditable empirical objects, discovering recurring hypothesis families, constructing executable signatures, and testing what survives on common empirical ground. The ontology is not fixed in advance.

Hard platform rule: hypothesis families, counts, variables, endpoints and signatures arise from the current corpus and declared question. No Alzheimer's ontology is embedded in the generic path.
