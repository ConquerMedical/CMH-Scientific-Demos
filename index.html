These tools have been evaluated on real research datasets. Interactive browser controls and illustrative visualizations are identified separately from frozen validation evidence. No page embeds protected engine logic, secret keys, patient data, or clinical treatment instructions.
Validation provenance. Each scientific demo now identifies the real dataset(s), analysis-specific sample or observation counts, and source citations supporting that product. Interactive browser scenarios are labeled separately from empirical validation. Counts use the scientifically appropriate unit—patients, states, recordings, neurons, perturbation-response observations, papers, or evaluation cells—and heterogeneous units are never added together.
